# Plan: polishing the forms and the dashboard UI

Status: **U0 to U5 built (2026-11-18); U6 not started.** See the progress notes after section 6. Requested 2026-11-18, after 1.4.0 shipped, as the work to do **before** new features: the edit forms do not look good and the spacing is uneven. This plan lists what is wrong, with evidence, and a small set of changes that fix it, in an order that ships useful improvements early. It is a UI-only plan: no route, no stored data and no behaviour of the database changes.

## 1. Goal

Make the screens people use to change data feel as careful as the preview step already does: forms with an even rhythm, one consistent set of sizes, controls that fit the kind of value they hold, and a sidebar and toolbar that do not wrap into clutter. Then go one step beyond fixing: use the forms to make mistakes harder (show what changed, say why a button is disabled) and make the app usable on a phone.

## 2. How this was reviewed (and what was not)

- **Method.** A planning pass using the design-critique rubric, in a single context. It is **not** a full `/impeccable critique` run: no isolated sub-agents, no saved score.
- **Measured in the running app.** DataCanvas started with `--enable-writes`, a local sample database (users and products with a few rows), a Chromium pane at 1280 by 800 and at 375 by 812. Sizes below were read from the page (`getBoundingClientRect` and computed styles), not estimated from screenshots. The row editor was also viewed in light theme.
- **Deterministic scan.** The design detector over `apps/web/features` and `apps/web/components` reported **no findings**. The problems below are about consistency and rhythm, which it does not look for.
- **Not reviewed.** The forms with many columns or very long values, the Overview, Paths, Health, Objects and Snapshots tabs, the canvas inspector, reduced-motion behaviour. Findings marked _(code)_ come from reading the source and should be confirmed when the work starts.
- **Isolation.** The review used a throwaway data folder and a throwaway database; nothing of the user's was touched.

## 3. What already works (keep it)

- The **preview dialog**: the exact statement in a monospace block with a copy button, a before and after table, warnings, and Apply that waits for a tick on destructive changes. It is the best screen in the product.
- Focus rings are visible everywhere checked, the labels are tied to their fields, and light and dark themes match.
- The builders use one control height (32 px inputs and selects) and the app's own `Input` component.
- The unlock dialog's role warning is clear and placed where the decision is made.

## 5. Design direction

One small set of rules, applied through shared pieces instead of per-file classes (today each form hand-rolls its label, hint and control classes).

- **Type scale for forms.** Section label 14 px/500, field label 13 px/500, control text 14 px (monospace only where the value is code or data), hint and error 12 px, and nothing below 11 px. The column type moves out of a 10 px badge into the label row at 12 px.
- **Rhythm.** 16 px between fields, 6 px from label to control, 4 px from control to hint, and **space for the hint always reserved or never**: either every field has a hint line or none does, so the pitch is constant. Dialog padding 24 px at 640 px and wider.
- **Three dialog sizes** as variants of `DialogContent`: small (448, confirmations and unlock), medium (640, row forms, structure, preview) and large (768, new table). Nobody writes a `max-w-*` in a feature file again.
- **Controls fit the value.** A one-line `Input` for numbers, dates, UUIDs and short text; a `textarea` only for text that can be long, JSON and values that already contain a line break, auto-sized and without the resize grip; a `Switch` or a two-option segmented control for booleans. Numeric inputs get the right `inputMode`.
- **NULL and default as part of the field.** A small "NULL" toggle inside the control's trailing edge (and "Default" where the column has one) replaces the radio triples and the lone checkbox. A required column simply shows its input.
- **Show the change.** A field that differs from what was loaded gets a left accent and a small reset button; the footer says "2 columns changed" and, when nothing changed, why Preview is disabled.
- **Footer stays put.** Body scrolls, header and footer do not.
- **Keyboard.** Focus the first editable non-key field (or the clicked cell's column). Ctrl or Cmd plus Enter opens the preview.

Everything keeps the accessible names the browser tests use (`Edit row N`, `Value for <column>`, `NULL for <column>`, `Database name`, and the rest) except where a control is deliberately replaced (the insert form's radios); the tests that name them are updated in the same change.

### Progress

**U0 (done).** `e2e/ui-forms.spec.ts` opens the seven data-changing dialogs in light and dark, saves a screenshot of each (`UI_FORMS_DIR`) and runs axe-core on them. Its first run found a real bug that is fixed: in light theme `--muted-foreground` was 4.3:1 on `--muted`, under the 4.5:1 minimum (the preview's before and after table header). `axe-core` is now an explicit dev dependency.

**U1 (done).** Shared pieces in `apps/web/components/ui`: `DialogContent` takes `size` (`confirm` 448, `form` 640, `builder` 768 px) and a sized dialog scrolls its body, keeps itself inside the window and pins its footer flush to the bottom edge; `FormField`, `WrappedField` and `FormStack` carry one type scale (section 14/500, label 13/500, meta and hint 12) and one rhythm (6 px label to control, 16 px between fields); `Textarea` and `Select` match `Input`. Every dialog in `features/writes` now uses them, and nothing in them is under 12 px. Closes findings 3 (in the dialogs), 8, 11, 12 and 15; findings 1, 2 and 4 to 10 are U2, 13 and 14 are U3, 16 to 18 are U4, 19 is U5. Rules in the spec fail the build if a dialog leaves the three widths, shows text under 11 px, lets the footer scroll away, or leaves a strip under it. The spec was run against the old build first and failed on exactly those points.

**U2 (done).** The row forms (`row-editor-dialog.tsx`, `insert-row-dialog.tsx`) share one `ValueField` (`value-field.tsx`): a switch for a boolean, a one-line field for a number, a date or a UUID, and a field that grows with its text for the rest (`controlKind` in `row-edit.ts`, tested). NULL and Default are small toggle buttons beside the control, and a required column with no default shows only its field. The add-row form's radios are gone, so the dialog no longer changes height as you work. In the editor a changed field is marked "Changed" with a Reset button in its label row, the footer says "N columns changed" or why Preview is waiting ("No changes yet"), focus starts on the first editable non-key column (`focusTarget`), and Ctrl or Cmd plus Enter previews from anywhere in either form. Every field is the same height and the same distance from the next (`FormField reserveHint`); a one-line `Textarea` is now exactly an `Input` tall (32 px). An untouched empty field is no longer reported as an error. Closes findings 1, 2, 3, 5, 6, 7, 9 and 10. **Not done:** finding 4 (full-width fields for short values, P3): a two-column layout for short types was left out because it would break the constant rhythm and the top-to-bottom reading order; revisit if forms feel too tall. The rules in `e2e/ui-forms.spec.ts` fail if a row form uses a radio or checkbox, loses its switch, focuses the key first, has an uneven pitch, shows an error before typing, or lets the add-row dialog change height. Axe found and fixed two more things on the way: the pressed Default and NULL buttons were dark text on a dark fill in dark theme, and the old dark-fill override that caused it is now spelled out in `ValueField`.

**U3 (done).** The builders' cards (`column-fields.tsx`, `foreign-key-fields.tsx`) sit on one 12-column grid with a header (the card's title, a "Can be NULL" toggle where it applies, and the remove button). In a column card the name, default and identity share the left edge and the type and comment share the right one; the foreign key card uses the same two columns (schema over column over on delete, table over points at over on update), so a foreign key's table starts exactly where a column's type starts. Array and Can be NULL are toggle buttons (`components/ui/toggle-button.tsx`, now shared with the row forms) instead of lone checkboxes; the "Columns" heading shows the count; the identity note moved under the Identity select. The cards stack on a narrow screen. Closes findings 13 and 14 (11, 12 and 15 were closed in U1). The rules in `e2e/ui-forms.spec.ts` fail if a card holds a checkbox, if the count is missing, or if any left edge differs within or between cards, including the foreign key card; the spec now also saves the bottom of a scrolling dialog so a long form can be checked by eye. Not done: the primary key and unique constraint lists are still rows of checkboxes (they are choices among the table's columns, not lone options), and a foreign key's "Points at" select is one column narrower than "Table" to leave room for the remove button on a second pair.

**U4 (done).** The sidebar's unlocked state is one line: the "Writes on" badge, then a lock button and a changes button (28 px icon buttons with tooltips and the same accessible names as before). The read-mode role note is one truncated line with a button that expands it and one that dismisses it. The Data toolbar is a bordered bar in three groups (Paging; Actions; View, pushed right) and stays within two lines at 1280 and 900 px. The row actions are 32 px targets with a tooltip (`components/ui/tooltip.tsx`, `Tip`); a blocked row's reason is the tooltip and the button's description. The unlock dialog asks its question once (the title) and lists Database, Connected to, Server reports and Role in one table. Closes findings 16 to 18. The rules in `e2e/ui-forms.spec.ts` fail if the question or database name is repeated, if the badge and buttons wrap, if a button is under 28 px (row actions 32), if the toolbar needs more than two lines, or if a tooltip is missing. Not done: the compact bar was only checked at the 288 px sidebar width on desktop; phones are U5.

**U5 (done).** Below 768 px the sidebar is a drawer (`components/layout/sidebar.tsx`, `stores/nav-store.ts`): a menu button in the header ("Open connections and tables", `mobile-nav-button.tsx`) slides it in over a dimmed page; picking a table or connection, Escape or a tap outside closes it. It is the same element as the desktop column, so connections and the tree exist once; closed it is `invisible`, so it is out of the tab order and hidden from screen readers. The view tabs scroll sideways with a fade at both edges, the Data toolbar is one scrolling row, and under 640 px every sized dialog is a full-screen sheet (`DialogContent`), with the footer still pinned. Closes finding 19. A browser test at 375 by 812 connects, opens the drawer, unlocks writes, finds a table, checks that the page never scrolls sideways, that the tabs and the toolbar scroll, and that the edit-row dialog fills the screen with Preview in view. Not done: the canvas, Overview, Paths, Health, Objects and Snapshots views were not checked on a phone, the dark theme was not looked at on a phone, and the connect dialog (unsized) was not made a sheet.

Decisions taken for U1: native selects kept (decision 3); the widths and type scale above. Other dialogs (connect, export, forget) are unchanged apart from a footer that now also sticks.

## 7. Risks

- **Breaking the tests that name controls.** The browser tests use accessible names. Mitigation: keep the names, and change a test only where a control is deliberately replaced.
- **Regressing the privacy rules.** `features/writes` must never use browser storage or the console, and a value must never be put in a title or tooltip. The rules in `privacy.test.ts` keep running; changed-field markers show values only inside the dialog the person typed them in.
- **Scope creep into a redesign.** This plan fixes consistency and adds a few aids to forms. It does not change the product's look, colours or layout of the canvas.
- **Native selects.** Keeping native `select` elements is cheaper and accessible, but they cannot be styled consistently across systems (see decision 3).

## 8. Open decisions

1. **How far U2 goes.** (a) Fix the form (single-line inputs, NULL toggle, change markers); (b) also add type-aware pickers (a date and time picker, a JSON editor). _Recommendation: (a)._ Pickers are a feature with their own accessibility cost; (a) removes the visible problems.
2. **Replace the insert form's radios.** _Recommendation: yes_, with an inline Default or NULL toggle. It removes the single-option radio and the layout jump. The radio browser tests change.
3. **Native selects or a styled select.** _Recommendation: keep native selects_ for now and fix their height, font and label; revisit if the builder grows.
4. **Version.** U1 to U4 are visual only. _Recommendation: 1.4.1 for U1 to U4 and 1.5.0 when U5 (phones) ships_, so the drawer is announced as a capability.
5. **Phones: how far.** (a) Make the app usable on a phone (U5); (b) leave phones as they are and say so. _Recommendation: (a)_, since the current state hides the whole sidebar. If the product is meant for desktops only, say that in the guide instead and skip U5.
6. **The other tabs.** Overview, Paths, Health, Objects and Snapshots were not reviewed. _Recommendation:_ review them after U1, when the shared pieces exist, in a short second plan rather than widening this one.
